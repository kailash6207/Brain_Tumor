How to Use NEUROSCAN AI

Follow the 4 clinical steps below to ingest brain MRI scans, contour lesions with sub-pixel active contours, extract high-order radiomics, and generate real-time WHO-CNS5 differential diagnoses.

STEP 1 📥

Ingest MRI Scan

Upload any brain scan (.dcm, .png, .jpg, .webp), press Ctrl+V to paste an image, load from URL, or pick from 12 pre-calibrated benchmark cases.

STEP 2 🎚️

Multi-Sequence & W/L

Switch sequences (T1+Gd, T2, FLAIR, DWI) and colormaps (Grayscale, Jet, Hot, Viridis). Apply Window/Level presets and measure tumor diameter with the Caliper tool.

STEP 3 ⚡

Auto-Detect & Pinpoint

Run saliency-guided active contour segmentation to isolate the tumor core, necrosis, and vasogenic edema. Or click anywhere on the MRI to pinpoint focal lesions.

STEP 4 📋

WHO-CNS5 Findings & Report

View primary diagnosis with WHO grade badge, confidence %, RECIST 1.1 mm axes, 3D volume (cm³), molecular biomarkers, and export a formatted hospital PDF report.
